**Every new name, species assignment, alliance, silhouette and ability below is an unapproved design proposal.** None updates Dragex canon or the existing 71-entry living roster. ## Art and review acceptance

One isolated complete subject, neutral background, no text or action effects; full horns, tails, feet and equipment visible. Tower images include the entire tower and modest base footprint, without landscape. A single consistent three-quarter view is safer than unverified multi-view sheets. Preserve recognizable anatomy; do not claim generated alternate views are geometrically matched. Compare each image to this brief and record real completion, defects and acceptance in the parent's review index. Reference art is for later Meshy work; no paid model generation authorized.
