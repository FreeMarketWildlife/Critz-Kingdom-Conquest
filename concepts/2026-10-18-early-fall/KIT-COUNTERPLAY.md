# Ten conqueror gameplay identities

**Original proposals; every number untested, 18 October 2026.** This is an initial kit differentiation pass to accompany the art, not an implementation or a claim of fair balance. All are unique first drinkers. Positions remain freely selectable; archetype baselines come from `systems.md`. Artwork depicts mundane equipment at rest; innate magic is allowed without turning equipment into powered machinery.

The passive/Q/W/E/R directions in the roster are expanded below. Use the final artwork manifest for exact names and visible props. Damage values are raw level-one starting values unless stated; skill scaling and attack animations still need prototype work. Do not add all suggested effects without a power budget review.

## Minion and tower implementation boundary

The ten allied minions are faction skins of shared Tier 1 melee/ranged templates at first. Their visible bells indicate reward tier; do not infer a large bell means more currency unless the template actually changes. Final equipment determines attack animation: a dagger carrier cannot use an unexplained sling projectile. If a ranged variant is later needed, author it separately with a visible bow, sling or innate spell pose.

Ten tower images fit five **visual** categories: scout, canopy, bastion, memorial/camp and marsh/storehouse. Proposed pairings: Reedwatch/Milepost; Shadepost/Reedwarden Belfry; Thornbell/Floodbrace; Ossuary/Barrowwatch; Huskhold/Miregate. All use the same range, target priority, collision footprint and HP by lane tier initially. Gate and stair placement are art choices; build a standardized collision mesh rather than collision from every decorative beam. Distinct faction towers can be recognized without making draft choice a hidden structure-stat advantage.

First evaluate each kit with one ability removed: does its silhouette and central decision still identify it? Then test full teams for control chaining, water dependence, free sustain and unavoidable approaches. The ten briefs deliberately avoid borrowing League kits, characters or assets; readable threat, hierarchy, opportunity cost and a useful opponent response are the shared MOBA design principles.
