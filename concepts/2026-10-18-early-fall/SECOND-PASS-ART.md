## Species and silhouette assessment

- **Conquerors:** Temba's large round ears, mottled coat and white tail tip distinguish African wild dog from the existing dire wolf. Eshe's dark elongated muzzle and striped haunches/legs distinguish okapi from Abe's giraffe; the upright body is a proposal, not canon animal anatomy. Orun's towering double-spiral horns and large oval shield differ materially from Serev's short pronged horns, slim spear and round shield. Both remain visibly bovid/ungulate fighters at a distance, so retain the horn contrast and different shield dimensions at gameplay LOD.
- **Tembo versus existing mammoth:** the selected elephant has smooth folded skin, enormous fan ears and exposed elephant head/trunk; there is no wool or mammoth hump. The distinction is clear. Shoulder shields and blanket hide torso/hip topology, which is ordinary occlusion, not a species failure.
- **Birds:** Veska's huge throat pouch and compact webbed-foot body read as pelican; Brann's barrel chest, barred brown wings, short bill and cheek whiskers read as bustard; Ivara's bald head, throat sac, heavy pointed bill and very long bare legs read as marabou. These three do not share a duplicate silhouette. Veska has deliberately anthropomorphic feathered grasping digits around the staff: model that choice consistently instead of inadvertently treating the staff as a fused wing bone.
- **Uld:** broad flat head, high-set eyes, heavy body and low limbs differentiate the proposal from a slim ordinary newt, but the overall silhouette will still evoke a giant salamander. Retain the broad head and avoid lightning/alchemist props so it does not become the already-listed giant-salamander concept. The full-resolution image shows three clearly exposed legs and one occluded far limb, not a verified four-view reconstruction. It is appropriately described as a stylized Koolasuchus proposal, not a scientific reconstruction or newly canon species.
- **Kavra/Huskcarrier:** Kavra's huge unequal claws, broad exposed armored back and indigo/orange surface separate it from Huskcarrier's cream spiral shell, red legs and high wicker load. The shell/body contrast is strong even in the contact sheet. Both need deliberate appendage reconstruction; the images do not prove hidden leg counts.
- **Small mammals:** Dustrunner and Dustfoot are the closest visual pair: warm tan upright mice, large round ears, pale belly, round shields and hooded cloth. Their weapon/shape distinction is useful (short dagger/short tail versus long spear/wicker buckler/long tail), but could vanish at top-down scale. Preserve Dustfoot's spear height and wicker shield silhouette and Dustrunner's compact dagger/solid shield. No regeneration needed for reference use; confirm identification at intended game zoom before shipping. Pouchrunner's narrow pointed nose/dark coat and Thorncourier's very long nose/huge ears already separate the shrew pair.

## Meshy handoff findings: normal limitations, not failed concepts

1. **Fine parts:** crab antennae, Ivara's toes, Thorncourier's tail/staff, long spear shafts, bell clappers, ropes and tower ladders are thin relative to bodies. Generate/model major bodies first where practical, then separate equipment and appendages. A single fused reconstruction will tend to merge these with adjacent surfaces.
2. **Occluded bodies:** Orun/Temba/Serev/Pouchrunner shields conceal arms or hands; Veska's folded wings conceal arm articulation; Tembo's blanket/shields conceal flank attachment. The images support appearance, not rear anatomy or animation-ready joints. Separate shield/staff meshes and design hidden limbs intentionally.
3. **Tower interiors:** Shadepost's staircase reaches a concealed cabin junction; Reedwarden Belfry's curved projecting balcony needs an underside/support decision; Milepost's ladder and railing leave interior stair/floor access hidden. These are believable concept structures, but neither the image nor its shadow establishes interior geometry. Resolve paths and supports on a blockout, with standardized gameplay collision independent of decorative overhangs.
4. **Style/scale:** towers vary substantially in width and height. That is useful faction differentiation, but the shared collision/range rules require normalized in-game bounds. The neutral backgrounds intentionally provide no common world-size ruler; assign explicit arena scales during import rather than inferring that a mouse is elephant-sized because both fill their files.
5. **Materials:** cloth fraying, fur strands, stone mortar, feather layering and crab surface bumps should largely become textures/normal detail. Keeping every visible strand as geometry would work against a clean readable game mesh.
6. **No multi-view promise:** all selected files are consistent single-view reference formats. Hidden backs/undersides and opposite equipment faces remain unshown. Do not market independent generated views as matched geometry if future views are added.

## Era and proposal boundary

Medieval spears, blades, shields, leather, cloth, rough masonry, thatch, timber and alarm bells fit the requested art direction. Fresh animal alliances building these works in the first approximately twenty years is an **authorized game/art assumption**, not evidence that Dragex canon has a documented early metallurgy or construction chronology. Nothing in the selected art requires excluding innate magic. No living Dragex/Moana image appears among the thirty, and no current image changes their sleep/death canon. The selected names and original personalities remain proposals, including the intentionally varied degree of anthropomorphism.

**Recommended disposition:** accept all thirty as reviewed concept references; fix the four textual/emblem points above in the handoff, retain species/occlusion caveats, and treat full ranged-wave variants plus controlled mesh/anatomy work as later production tasks. No paid Meshy work is implied or authorized.
