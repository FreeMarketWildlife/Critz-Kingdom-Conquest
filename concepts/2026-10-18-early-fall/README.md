## Proposal and continuity status

All new individuals, factions, abilities, map geometry and numerical values are **unapproved, untested proposals**. Current Dragex canon governs. The requested era is anchored to the first bloodwater drinking and approximately twenty following years, without changing the global game-age registry or calendar mapping. The existing 71-entry roster, including historical-person-inspired choices and pending names, remains unchanged.

Current canon has Dragex asleep and Moana deceased; the older live-dragon boss direction is preserved as a documented conflict. This pack proposes nonliteral, match-only trials at the established mountain/bay associations. Likewise, a medieval sling/stone-thrower is a proposed era variant of existing Tier 2 cannon wording, not a silent canon rewrite. Innate magic is allowed; the art excludes technological magical weapons, guns, steampunk and futuristic equipment.

## Review and production limits

Individual references use neutral studio backgrounds and one complete subject in a single three-quarter view. Modelers must resolve hidden surfaces, far limbs, thin tails, bird wing/gripping anatomy and equipment intersections. These are not geometry-matched turnarounds, finished meshes, rigging proofs or tested gameplay. Use an individual image for later image-to-3D; never upload the contact sheet as one model reference. No Meshy models or credits were purchased, and no paid Meshy stages were run.

No source roster, gameplay code, private manuscript or unrelated project was edited. Only the new dated concept directory was added to Kingdom Conquest. A preservation record documents the pre-existing changed tracked files. No public hosting, access changes, commit or push was performed. The gallery is a local review artifact; the ZIP contains the same thirty selected images and design files.
