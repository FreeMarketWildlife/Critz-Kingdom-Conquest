# Modeling and reference handoff

**New production proposals, 18 October 2026.** These artworks are design references, not approved canon, finished meshes or geometry turnarounds. No Meshy model generation or credit purchase was performed.

Use each individual PNG as a **single-view image-to-3D reference**. Each depicts one isolated subject in a neutral studio setting, with a readable whole silhouette. Do not pass the contact sheet or gallery screenshot as model input. They contain multiple subjects. The chosen three-quarter view communicates volume; unseen backs, undersides, inner joints and shield reverse faces remain unresolved. Do not claim dimensional agreement between independently generated views. If multiview modeling is required, first approve one mesh/blockout and derive orthographic renders of that same geometry.

Suggested review scale is relative rather than biological: conqueror median standing height 2 m; minion 0.7 m; watchtower 8 m. These are **game production starting assumptions**, not Libriam biology or canon measurements. Keep broad species cues at gameplay camera distance. Conquerors must retain their distinguishing horns, bills, ears, skull shapes or carapaces when equipment is simplified. Tower gates must remain readable for minion departure; decoration must not obscure targetable footprint.

For later Meshy work, upload a selected individual image, review the preview before any additional paid stage, and inspect topology, feet/contact points, equipment intersections, symmetry and silhouette manually. Separate thin weapons, shields and hanging bells into intentional objects after generation. Do not infer an image proves riggability or topology quality. Crab leg articulation and bird wing/hand anatomy need particular attention. The proposed coconut crab is a deliberate small-body conqueror exception; approve arena scale rather than enlarge its supposed natural biology.

For rigging: establish neutral rest pose, ground plane and forward axis, check all limbs, then test idle, walk, attack and hit reaction. Treat all painted undersides and occluded joints as decisions still required. Avoid baking cast shadows into albedo. Keep subject materials clean: wood, stone, iron, leather, linen, rope and animal surfaces. Intrinsic Feysian magic may be represented through animation/VFX later; these clean references intentionally omit active spells.

## Art direction

Original stylized medieval animal fantasy. The first river-drinkers form provisional alliances over shelter, routes and water; their personalities are proposals. No arcane machinery, firearms, powered armor, gears, future devices or magical technological weapons. Natural magical ability remains allowed. Minion bells are physical affiliation/reward markers, not batteries. New towers are variants of a small shared set of gameplay categories; ten art skins need not create ten separate balance systems.

Clarity goals draw from Riot's [Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/): recognizable silhouettes, readable facing, visual hierarchy and restrained noise. Application here is original: species anatomy carries recognition, simple equipment signals purpose, and the most consequential attacks receive the strongest telegraph. No League character or asset is used as a reference image.
