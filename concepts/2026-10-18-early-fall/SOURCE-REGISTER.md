## External design references

Riot's [Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) and [Champion Counterplay](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-may-14/) were used for general readable MOBA principles: silhouette recognition, meaningful hierarchy, reduced noise and useful opponent responses.
