# Critz Kingdom Conquest — vision & lore

## Confirmed
- Single-map competitive MOBA influenced by League's top-down camera and familiar controls. No ARAM. Land-based battlefield between kingdoms, not a floating island.
- Bottom: **Morane Bay** with **Moana** (dragon-like objective). Top: **Mount Yokai** with **Dragex** (Baron-like objective).
- **Conquerors** are unique named animals, recognizable down to species where possible; degree of anthropomorphism varies. Usually one individual per species initially.
- Basic attack, passive, and Q/W/E/R for every Conqueror.
- Minion last hits earn **bells**, and minions wear visible bells; stronger/higher-value minions can visibly carry more.
- Conqueror-specific allied small creatures reflect lore alliances, not simply biological groupings.
- 3D stylized fantasy, possibly more cartoonish than League, with potential model reuse from Critz Battlefront.

## World lore
- **Dragex** is spelled D-R-A-G-E-X.
- Fae-blooded animals are **Feysian** (dictated spelling F-E-Y-S-I-A-N; pronunciation transcription uncertain).
- Fairy blood from battles atop Mount Yokai entered a river. Animals that drank became more anthropomorphic/Fae-blooded, not newly sentient.
- Ancient male giant Galápagos tortoise was among the earliest drinkers. His Greek-ancient-derived name has NOT been accurately captured by dictation. **Name pending typed confirmation.**

## Tentative game systems
- One or two elemental/terrain **types** independent of combat class, unique passive, and abilities. E.g. Water type grants river movement speed; Grass faster in brush; Flying can cross walls.
- Flight stamina idea for Eagle balance.
- Map skins representing different Dragex kingdoms.
- Relative team coloring (allies blue, enemies red) under consideration.
- Some semi-aquatic Conquerors benefit dramatically from the central river. Most fully aquatic species excluded, with potential deliberate exception for a land-capable octopus.

## Principles
- Inspirations from people/other fiction serve as creative starting points. Avoid directly copying protected designs/likenesses in commercially released assets without appropriate clearance.
- **Confirmed** = user decision; **Provisional** = unsettled user choice; **Suggested** = brainstorm, not approved.
