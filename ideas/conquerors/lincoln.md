# Lincoln — the Iron Fist

Confirmed creator decisions from the character interview. Playable Conqueror name: **Lincoln**. Epithet: **the Iron Fist**. No ability kit has been approved yet.

## Identity and history

Lincoln is an intelligent, English-speaking male silverback gorilla and a first-generation Feysian: he personally drank fairy-blood-bearing river water after the battle atop Mount Yokai. He rules the jungles surrounding Mount Yokai in Hokkaido, where Dragex lives. This is a pre-civilization setting; he fell into leadership rather than inheriting a dynasty.

Jungle creatures and birds respect his tremendous power. He ruled successfully for a long time, defending the weak and the righteous. Historically he never left his jungle kingdom and never desired to rule the rest of Hokkaido. His participation in matches need not literally reproduce this territorial restriction.

He submits to the will of the Creator. He treats allies extremely well, but judges allies and enemies alike under the Creator's law. He kills those he regards as evildoers, including for theft or even the smallest wrongdoing. His defining flaw is his inability to show mercy. This is his characterization, not an approved gameplay execution mechanic.

## Temperament and fighting fantasy

Noble, righteous, confident and proud without the creator intending corrupt pride. Do not reduce him to a hot-tempered rage character. He does not need others' approval or care what they think of him because of his power. He rules with an iron fist.

An unstoppable silverback and a brawler. His strength is primarily physical. He had an opportunity to harness elemental powers and briefly dabbled in darkness, but rules through pure strength. Whether that past has any present visual or gameplay expression is undecided.

## Voice, presence and inspirations — interview update

- Very articulate; voice delivery favors short commanding phrases rather than extended dialogue.
- Present power comes from pure physical strength.
- Enemies should fear facing him one-on-one. Allies see his presence as a deterrent that can drive enemies away and may prefer him concealed to set up a bait or ambush. This is the intended player feeling, not a confirmed stealth mechanic or guaranteed duel win.
- Fighting and visual inspirations: Volibear's downward slamming motion, the Hulk, and King Kong. Specific abilities have not been chosen.
- Bronze armor and teal cloth may have been found or gifted; their origin remains undecided.
- No additional must-keep design details or exclusions were specified yet.

## Combat kit interview — creator direction

These are approved qualitative directions, not balanced implementation specs. Numerical values and interaction rules remain open.

### Movement and combat posture

Normal locomotion is knuckle-walking on all fours, with King Kong as a reference. He rises taller when combat starts to deliver towering hammer blows; do not give him a continuously upright humanoid fighting style. Attack pace is steady and forceful. He absorbs damage through toughness. Short reach and movement speed available only in bursts are intended weaknesses.

### Basic attacks and passive

Repeating sequence: right-hand swing, left-hand swing, then a two-handed downward slam with a small area of effect. The third-hit sequence is intended as his passive. First two hits have sharp impact; the third has a deeper thud and restrained ground cracking. Basic attacks must not have excessive effects. Ability impacts may crumble the ground and leave a temporary visual impact marker; actual terrain alteration is not decided.

### Q — grab and punch (name undecided)

At close range, grab an enemy with the left hand, then automatically deliver basic attacks with the right hand while grappling. Desired result is substantial damage. Grapple duration, escape rules, movement restrictions and passive interaction are undecided.

### W — movement burst (name undecided)

A burst of movement speed that lets Lincoln run up to an enemy. Other effects are undecided.

### E — leap and slam (name undecided)

Leap into the air and slam down, slowing enemies. If used during Q, leap while carrying the grabbed enemy and slam them down for extra damage. The Q–E combination is a signature spectacle. It should have a substantial mana cost; exact resource rules and costs remain undecided.

### R — Wrath (current direction)

An empowerment ultimate that strengthens all his abilities, expressing righteous anger and an intense powered-up state. Specific enhancements, visuals and duration are undecided. The earlier feet-grab/backflip throw ultimate was superseded by this direction; do not implement it as his current ultimate. Biting/ripping were brainstormed before that decision, not selected abilities.

### Role and combat progression

Top lane is the primary envisioned role. Jungle is also envisioned because he can gank; support is an optional possibility. The creator initially suggested short trades, then reconsidered toward growing stronger in extended fights and winning sustained duels. Exact combat ramp-up mechanic remains open.

Darkness might subtly contribute after roughly ten seconds of combat, but this is tentative brainstorming, not an approved passive or timer. Reconcile any such mechanic with physical strength and righteous Wrath before implementation. No special post-kill behavior is requested.

### Next questions to resolve

Grapple counterplay and duration; Q punch/passive interaction; E aiming and interruptibility; Wrath enhancements and resource; attack-chain reset rules and area damage; durability and sustained-combat growth. Do not invent final numbers without playtesting.

## Kit refinement — latest creator decisions

This section supersedes conflicting earlier interview details. Keep the established spelling **Lincoln**; later voice transcription “Linken” is not an explicit rename.

### Proposed one-word names

Creator delegated naming. Passive: **Impact**. Q: **Clench**. W: **Pursuit**. E: **Quake**. R: **Wrath** (creator-selected).

### Impact — passive

Normal attacks alternate right swing, left swing, then a two-handed AoE slam. The third hit scales with Lincoln's own maximum health. Exact coefficient and whether this is bonus damage remain open. Q punches do not trigger the third-hit passive; whether they preserve or reset the normal attack counter is undecided.

### Clench — Q

A short, timed channel: left hand reaches toward the victim's neck and holds them in place. Visual lift is intended, but can be modest and adapted to target anatomy. Repeated right-handed basic-attack punches occur at Lincoln's attack speed, so greater attack speed yields more punches during the same channel. The hold lowers the target's armor/defenses; amount, application timing, stacking and persistence are undecided.

The victim cannot fight back, auto-attack or escape through button mashing. Creator calls it a snare rather than a stun; implement the explicit grab restrictions rather than assuming standard snare behavior. Exact casting restrictions and cleansing interactions remain to be clarified. Duration is short, approximately 1–2 seconds, with two seconds the intended maximum; final value undecided. Pressing Q again can end the channel. Lincoln's movement during the channel and interruptibility of Q itself remain undecided.

### Pursuit — W

Burst of movement speed to close distance. No additional effect confirmed.

### Quake — E

Leap and slam with a slow. Lincoln is unstoppable during the leap. During Q, he carries the captive and slams them beneath/immediately in front of himself, without a throw-away displacement. Q–E deals extra damage and is intended to have substantial mana cost. Exact meaning of unstoppable regarding damage and target interactions remains to be specified; do not equate it with invulnerability.

### Wrath — R active

Available on cooldown without requiring a meter. Lincoln becomes darker with a subtle dark aura and more focused, not out of control. On activation, fears enemy Conquerors in a generous surrounding radius; they slowly flee. Grants movement speed and attack speed, and adds magic damage to his abilities. Whether this converts existing damage or adds separate damage, and whether ordinary attacks gain magic damage, remain undecided. No toughness buff or special third-hit amplification from R is confirmed. Third-hit scaling belongs to Impact and ordinary maximum-health growth.

### Wrath — R passive stacks

Whenever an allied Conqueror dies within the same radius used by Wrath's fear, Lincoln gains a Wrath stack. Stacks have no cap. Activating R consumes all stored stacks, strengthening that activation; consumed stacks are gone. Exact boosted stats, per-stack scaling, duration and fear interaction remain undecided. Unlock timing, own-death persistence, vision requirements and qualifying deaths remain open. This replaces any assumption that combat time or an automatic ten-second darkness trigger is the selected ramp-up mechanic.

## Interaction rules — latest interview update

These decisions supersede earlier open questions.

- **Clench movement:** Lincoln is stationary during the channel unless Wrath is active. With Wrath active, he may move while holding the target. Movement speed and interaction with Wrath expiring mid-grab remain unspecified.
- **Clench interruption:** Hard crowd control and silence interrupt the channel before the leap. Slow-only effects are not confirmed interrupters. Exact enumeration of hard-CC effects remains an implementation detail to define.
- **Armor reduction:** Each Clench punch reduces the captive's armor further; there is no immediate reduction on grabbing. Amount, reduction floor and duration after release remain open.
- **Quake landing:** Releases the captive upon landing.
- **Impact counter:** Clench neither advances nor resets the ordinary three-hit attack counter. Normal attacks resume from the preserved position.
- **Wrath duration:** Ten seconds, independent of stacks.
- **Wrath stacks:** Strengthen attack speed, movement speed and magic damage. Also increase fear duration. Tentative increment: approximately 0.1 seconds of additional fear per stack, not a locked balance value. The proposed one-second increment was explicitly rejected as too strong. Base fear duration, scaling coefficients and any cap on fear duration remain undecided; stored stack count remains uncapped.
- **Death:** Lincoln loses all stored Wrath stacks on death (final decision after reconsideration).
- **Wrath activation:** Explosive King Kong-like roar and a few rapid chest beats, followed immediately by combat. Creator intends instantaneous activation; do not infer a mandatory long cast or lockout from the animation. Exact animation blending and presentation timing remain open.

Creator voice transcription used “clinch” in this update. The previously assigned ability name **Clench** is retained pending an explicit naming change.
