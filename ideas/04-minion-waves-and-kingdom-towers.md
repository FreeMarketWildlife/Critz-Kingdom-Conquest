# Lane Waves, Barracks, and Kingdom Towers — v0.2

## Confirmed structure names
- **Base** = the Base.
- **Castle** = nexus-equivalent, final structure. Earlier wording indicates minions originate from the Castle, but latest specification says Tier 1 exits lane outer towers; reconcile Castle's exact production role later.
- **Barracks** = inhibitor-equivalent; destroying an enemy Barracks permits powerful **Tier 3 siege minions** to spawn for the attacking side.
- Towers are medieval watchtowers, with small gates at their bases.

## Kingdom identity
- **Five Conquerors per team**.
- Each Conqueror is associated with a lore-defined **Kingdom**.
- **Three** of the five Conquerors bring their Kingdoms into each match, linked to the team's towers and minion armies.
- There will be **about five different tower types**; every Conqueror maps to one of these categories; definitions TBD.
- Each Kingdom's towers spawn its own specific critter/minion variants. Distinguish a Kingdom from a tower-type category until specified.

## Clarifications — creator confirmed
- **Tier 3 siege machines spawn from the Castle**, not the Barracks. Destruction of the opposing Barracks enables them; exact wave frequency/quantity still TBD.
- Each team has five roles assigned **before** the match: **Top, Mid, Bot, Support, Jungle**.
- **Any Conqueror may be selected for any role**; roles aren't hard-locked by character.
- The **Top laner's Kingdom determines Top Lane towers and minion faction; Mid laner's Kingdom determines Mid; Bot laner's Kingdom determines Bot**.
- **Support and Jungle Conquerors DO NOT influence tower/Kingdom choice**.
- Kingdom affiliation belongs to the Conqueror, not to the position permanently: the same Conqueror can influence a different lane's towers when assigned that role in another match.
- Previously unresolved question of how three Kingdoms are selected is now **RESOLVED**.
