# Character kits and inspiration decisions

Every Conqueror tracks: exact species, name, gender, anthropomorphism, role, type(s), innate type bonuses, basic attack, passive, Q, W, E, R, allied minions, lore, visual concept. Do not convert assistant proposals into decided mechanics.

## Jon Author (aardvark)
- John MacArthur-inspired smart priest/cleric. **Johnny Mac** skin wears suit/tie with recognizably pastor-inspired look.
- **Basic:** whacks with an actual bound hardcover **book** (not scroll).
- **Passive:** **Sound Doctrine** — name chosen, mechanics TBD.
- **Expository Teaching:** reduces enemy defenses; Q slot **proposed**, not locked.
- **Bible Bump:** strong book whack, possibly W; stun/synergy only suggested.
- **E:** **Laying on of Hands** — extremely short-range point-and-click heal, possibly charge longer to restore larger **% max HP**.
- **R:** **Excommunication** — name fixed, exact effect not determined.
- **Apostolic Authority** explicitly rejected.

## Confirmed mechanics of other Conquerors
- Ancient Galápagos tortoise: biting basic; tank, psychic abilities; ancient lore.
- Grizzly: melee-only lightning claws, burst speed, tank. Absolutely no ranged attacks.
- Dire wolf: fast assassin, not tank.
- Eagle: strong claw damage and tornado wind; squishy; wall traversal, maybe stamina; wall traversal might be type bonus, NOT unique passive.
- Panther: brush leap passive concept, bites, another leap, maybe climb walls.
- Giraffe / Abe: quadrupedal; unusually long-range head/neck sweep auto, potential AOE.
- Moose: charges with substantial burst and minion collision ghosting.
- Crocodile: boomerang fighter, relatively slow/tanky, speed bursts.
- T. rex: biting ultimate devours; successful eating grows it bigger and bigger.
- Spinosaurus: movement faster in water, regardless of type-system outcome.
- Triceratops: charge + substantial tankiness.
- Parasaurolophus: sound waves.
- Wolverine: berserker, Napoleon-like bicorne military commander, NOT pirate.
- Hyena: boosted stats near allied Conquerors; lifesteal uncertain.
- Sloth: slower than ancient tortoise, tree mobility; possible Yuumi-like attach support.
- Bison: upright large durable slow bruiser; double-sided axe uncertain.
- Gorilla: physical Hulk-style brute, NOT fire.
- Komodo dragon: venomous **on-hit melee passive** + charging ability (slot unknown).
- Alligator snapping turtle: slower on land / faster in water, invisible when resting under muddy water out of combat; lunges out for huge execute damage.
- King cobra: Medusa-like ultimate.
- Giant anteater: **passive** extra damage to minions and max-health-percentage heal on last hits.
- Platypus: **critical hit** uses venomous heel spur and powerful damage over time. Biological note: male platypuses are the venom-spurred ones; earlier female gender suggestion should not be presumed final.
- Kangaroo: female, joey in pouch, kicks.
- Peacock: butterfly allies.
- Eagle and Moose may ignore minion collisions (eagle under consideration).
