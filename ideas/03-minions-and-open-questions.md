# Minions and open questions

## Confirmed minion concepts
Each Conqueror is associated with animal minions according to Dragex lore; their species and allegiance are not automatically determined by real biological taxonomy. Minions wear visible bells corresponding to value/strength, and last-hits award bells.

| Ancient tortoise's affiliated minion | Role | Equipment / damage |
|---|---|---|
| Basilisk lizard | Warrior | Long sword, ice damage |
| Frog | Melee | Scimitar or katana, psychic/physical damage |
| Fire salamander | Mage | Fire spells/damage |
| Small turtle | Tank/heavy | Cannonball projectile or small catapult, weapon undecided |

Amphibians are often mages in Dragex, but swordsman frogs are a named exception. The name **Koric** is a salamander in related lore, **NOT** an approved Conqueror for this game. Pythons are **not** automatically allied with other reptiles despite classification.

**Trump-inspired peacock:** affiliated minions include butterflies.

## Candidate minions (creator invited independent brainstorming, NOT yet assigned)
- Insects/arthropods: ants, beetles, praying mantises, butterflies, moths, black widow spiders, scorpions. (Spiders and scorpions are arachnids.)
- Reptiles: geckos, chameleons, skinks, tiny turtles and lizards.
- Amphibians: fire salamanders, newts, frogs, toads.
- Small mammals: mice, rats, moles, shrews, squirrels, chipmunks, hedgehogs.
- Small birds: sparrows, finches, kingfishers, hummingbirds, magpies.

## Questions / risks
1. Are personal minions lane-wave replacements, summons that follow Conquerors, tower guardians, or a combination?
2. How are minion configurations resolved for teams with five different Conquerors?
3. Do different minion species have distinct combat stats or cosmetic equivalents?
4. Is the proposed type system (Water/Grass/Flying etc.) global or selective? How is wall flight balanced?
5. Which Conquerors are in launch MVP versus longer-term backlog?
6. Choose taxonomic species for ambiguous animals, and finalize character gender/name spelling.
7. Avoid overloading overlap: grizzly/Teddy bear; base eagle/harpy eagle/Washington bald eagle; chimp/bonobo Rogan/Caesar; bison/ox/yak/buffalo.
8. How does octopus remain mobile and combat-capable on land? How do walrus and seal navigate lanes?
9. What can Kiwi Ray Comfort do while retaining recognizable kiwi identity at small scale?
10. How does stacking T. rex growth maintain fair pathing/collision?
11. What is the ancient tortoise's EXACT typed name? **Do not silently assume.**
12. Review IP and right-of-publicity implications before publishing recognizable real-person/fictional-character skins.

## Confirmed exclusions
No ARAM, no whales/fully aquatic animals as ordinary roster additions, no domesticated animals; octopus deliberately singled out for exception.
