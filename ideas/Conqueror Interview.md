# Conqueror Interview

Reusable creator interview for every Conqueror in Critz Kingdom Conquest. Reference this file when starting a character or resuming their design. The aim is a distinct character, coherent playable kit and concrete animation brief, not merely a completed questionnaire.

## How to conduct the interview

Read the character's existing notes, world lore and roster first. Reuse answered facts; do not ask the creator to repeat them. Ask manageable rounds of related questions, adapt to voice answers, and follow interesting specifics with fresh character-specific questions. Offer examples as suggestions, not approved choices. Preserve uncertainty and the creator's final corrections. Do not silently turn brainstorming into mechanics.

After each round, save confirmed decisions, tentative ideas, superseded concepts and unanswered questions in that Conqueror's file. Keep naming consistent despite voice-transcription variations. Summarize what changed briefly, then ask the next useful round. When paused, save the exact pending questions and stop dependent work.

All Conquerors have a basic attack, passive and Q/W/E/R. Current lore identifies playable Conquerors as first-generation Feysian animals who personally drank fairy-blood-bearing river water. Do not invent later-generation lore. Character history and match mechanics need not reproduce each other literally.

## Identity, lore and personality

- Name, title, species, gender and age? Which are confirmed or intentionally undecided?
- What is the fantasy of being this character in one sentence?
- Homeland, people, allies and position in Hokkaido? How did they become a leader or fighter?
- What do they want, protect, fear and refuse to do? Why participate in conquest?
- Relationship to the Creator, fairy blood and elemental power?
- Defining virtue, flaw, contradiction and formative event?
- Treatment of allies, strangers and enemies? What challenges their convictions?
- Intelligence, speech style, humor and emotional range?
- Meaning of clothing, armor, weapons, scars and symbols?
- Inspirations and the specific qualities to borrow? What must stay or be avoided?

## Battlefield identity and uniqueness

- Primary role and optional roles? Target players and difficulty?
- Short trades, sustained duels, team fights, ambushes, protection or objectives?
- What should allies and opponents feel on seeing them?
- Signature mechanic or memorable moment? What gameplay choices does it create?
- Reliable strengths, exploitable weaknesses and clear counterplay?
- How does this kit differ from existing Conquerors? What overlap is intentional?
- What species-specific anatomy, senses or behavior can make the kit distinctive?
- Which character beliefs or habits should appear in combat, without forcing literal lore mechanics?

## Movement and basic attacks

- Normal gait, combat stance, idle behavior and transitions?
- Turning, stopping, accelerating and chasing? Burst speed versus sustained speed?
- Attack limb or weapon, range, rhythm, sequence and alternate motions?
- Windup, contact and recovery? Which moments must read from the top-down camera?
- Attack-speed scaling: how does the motion remain clear at high speeds?
- On-hit effects, third-hit sequences, counters and reset rules?
- How do attacks behave during movement, other abilities and against unusual target shapes?
- Impact sound, recoil, hit pause, effects and restrained versus spectacular presentation?

## Passive and each ability

For passive, Q, W, E and R, ask only the applicable questions:

- One-word working name, purpose and fantasy? Active, passive, toggle, channel or recast?
- Targeted, directional, ground-targeted or self-cast? Range, area and aiming rules?
- Exact sequence from input through windup, hit, recovery and completion?
- Damage type and scaling? What receives damage and what does not?
- Resource, cooldown, charges, duration and costs? Keep balance values provisional until tested.
- Movement, attack and casting restrictions for caster and target?
- Crowd control: precise effects rather than a label alone? Duration, immunity, cleansing and interruption?
- What ends the effect: time, recast, death, range, obstruction or another action?
- Can it miss? What happens on failure? How can an opponent respond?
- Effects on minions, jungle monsters, objectives, allies and structures?
- Interaction with attack counters, on-hit effects, other abilities and items?
- What visibly and audibly warns opponents? Which effects persist afterward?

## Combos, resources and edge cases

- Signature combo and intended order? Which transitions need dedicated animations?
- Captured or displaced targets: attachment point, pose, escape and release location?
- Target anatomy and size differences? Adaptation or fallback for creatures without the expected limbs?
- Channels, leaps and transformations: interruptible phases versus unstoppable phases? Unstoppable is not automatically invulnerable.
- Stack sources, consumption, caps, decay, death persistence and unlock timing?
- What happens if a buff expires mid-action, either participant dies or a target becomes untargetable?
- How do cooldowns and mana prevent repeated lockdown or unavoidable damage?
- Are allies rewarded for cooperation? Could a mechanic encourage undesirable play, such as farming allied deaths?
- Unlimited scaling: which effects remain readable and fair, and which need separate caps?

## Ultimate and growth

- Ultimate's signature spectacle and tactical purpose?
- Instant activation or cast time? Movement allowed during presentation?
- Changes to each ability, basic attacks, stats, posture and materials?
- Duration and cooldown? Stack-dependent changes versus fixed behavior?
- Early, middle and late-game strengths? Intended build choices and tradeoffs?
- What can opponents see, anticipate, evade or wait out?

## Visuals, voice and animation brief

- Top-down silhouette, palette, materials and team readability?
- Elemental or supernatural effects: colors, source, intensity and lore meaning?
- Ground marks, trails and environmental effects: visual only or gameplay changes?
- Voice delivery, ability calls, exertion, hits and reactions? Avoid inventing final dialogue without discussing tone.
- Idle, locomotion, three attack phases, abilities, combo transitions, hit reactions and death?
- Recall, spawn, victory and optional emotes only where useful?
- Root motion versus in-place motion, contact points and target-dependent animations?
- Minimum prototype clips versus polish priorities? Rig limitations and needed bones or controls?

## Ready-to-prototype checkpoint

Before building, summarize the confirmed kit and outstanding choices. Confirm that targeting, timing, movement restrictions, counterplay, interactions and visual tells are sufficiently defined for the intended prototype. Separate animation needs from numerical balance experiments. Do not require every lore detail to be finished before useful prototyping.
