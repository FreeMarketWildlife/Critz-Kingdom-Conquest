# World, Lore, Map, and Character Inspiration

## Map / world
- The world is **Dragex** (D-R-A-G-E-X). MOBA arena is a grounded kingdom vs kingdom war (not a floating island).
- Map **top**: Mount Yokai, with **Dragex** serving as Baron-style neutral boss.
- Map **bottom**: Morane Bay, with **Moana** serving as dragon-style neutral boss.
- River traverses the battlefield. Additional kingdom-inspired visual skins proposed. Exact lane layout, matchmaking and team size TBD.

## Fae-blooded origins
- Fairies fought atop Mount Yokai; their blood entered a river descending the mountain.
- Animals drinking it became anthropomorphic/powerful, not merely sentient.
- **Feysian** is spelled F-E-Y-S-I-A-N by creator (voice dictation sometimes yields Feyjian), meaning Fae-blooded animals.
- Ancient male Galápagos tortoise was among earliest transformed; exact character name spelling TBD.

## Development safeguards
- Real-world people and famous fictional franchises are creative prompts, not a license to copy protected character designs or exploit living persons' likeness commercially. Review names, public-figure appearance, skins and trademark risks before publishing.
- Preserve 'confirmed', 'proposed' and 'rejected' statuses. Future unique ability kits should be built around original Critz identity.
