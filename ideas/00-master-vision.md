# Critz Kingdom Conquest — Master Vision

Status conventions: **Confirmed** = explicitly decided; **Proposed** = under consideration; **TBD** = unresolved. Preserve user-dictated name spellings as provisional unless explicitly confirmed.

## Core
- **Confirmed:** Competitive animal-themed MOBA set in Dragex (D-R-A-G-E-X). Playable unique named animals are called **Conquerors**. A character's real species should be identifiable. Usually one individual per species initially.
- **Confirmed direction:** League-like top-down view and familiar MOBA controls, one primary map/mode (no ARAM), two kingdoms at war on land, not floating islands.
- **Proposed:** Cartoonier 3D graphics, possibly reuse models from Critz Battlefront; alternative kingdom-themed map skins.
- Characters may be strongly or subtly anthropomorphic, including fully quadrupedal giraffe.
- **Confirmed:** Lane-minion currency is **bells** rather than gold. Bell objects visible on minions indicate relative strength/reward. Example heavy turtle minion might wear five bells; exact reward counts TBD.
- **Confirmed:** Conquerors have basic attack, passive, Q/W/E/R, associated minion factions shaped by in-world politics/lore rather than simple taxonomy.
- **Proposed type system:** one or two terrain/element types independent of roles/passives: Water grants speed in water, Grass speed in brush, Flying can traverse walls (possibly limited stamina). Not yet finalized. Spinosaurus river speed is separately confirmed.
- Prefer wild, land-capable animals, no domesticated animals. Semi-aquatic exceptions allowed, including an explicitly approved inventor octopus. Age and scale can justify very different real-life body sizes in one arena.
- Character inspiration can come from historical figures, modern people or fiction; establish original Critz identities, and review publicity/IP rights before commercializing close likenesses.
